Privacy Policy

App: Agrisahay Mechanic

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Agrisahay Mechanic is used by mechanics and administrators to manage mechanic registration, profile verification, job assignments, community announcements, and support communication.

Information we collect

We may collect and store the following information when you use the app:

  • Phone number used for login and OTP verification.
  • Profile details such as full name, village, district, state, pincode, address, age, and years of experience.
  • Account status, verification status, and profile update request details.
  • Job assignment details, job status, and job history.
  • Community announcement interaction data needed to show unread announcements.
  • Device notification token if push notifications are enabled on your device.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Verify mechanic accounts and allow secure login.
  • Show profile information to authorized administrators for approval and updates.
  • Assign jobs and track job status.
  • Send important app notifications and community announcements.
  • Provide support and maintain app security.

Data storage and security

App data is stored using Firebase and Google Cloud services. We use access controls and Firebase security rules to help protect user data from unauthorized access.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with authorized app administrators and service providers needed to operate the app, such as Firebase and Google Cloud.

Data retention

We keep account, profile, and job information as long as needed to operate the service, meet business requirements, resolve issues, or comply with legal obligations.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can request profile correction or account/data deletion by contacting support. See our Data Deletion Instructions.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for mechanics and administrators. It is not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page.
