Data Deletion Instructions

App: Agrisahay Mechanic

Effective date: 21 September 2026

You can request deletion of your Agrisahay Mechanic account data by contacting Agrisahay support.

How to request deletion

  1. Call Agrisahay support at 9646424964.
  2. Provide the mobile number used for your Agrisahay Mechanic account.
  3. Tell support that you want your account and profile data deleted.

What data may be deleted

After verifying the request, we may delete or anonymize mechanic account data including phone number, profile details, profile update requests, notification tokens, and active app account records.

Data we may retain

Some job assignment history, audit records, or records required for business, safety, dispute resolution, or legal compliance may be retained where necessary.

Processing time

Deletion requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable time after verification.
